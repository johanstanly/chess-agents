# Magnus's notebook: lessons from my earlier games

1. Before pushing a pawn, check if it releases tension or lets an opponent piece escape a pin/attack—calculate captures first.
2. When ahead in material, avoid grabbing extra pawns if it lets the opponent's passed pawn or king activity become dangerous—prioritize safety and simplification.
3. Before an attacking queen move, verify it isn't answered by a simple capture that refutes the whole idea; check opponent's best response first.
