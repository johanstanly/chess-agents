# Magnus's notebook: rules from my earlier games

1. [endgame] In rook endings, before every rook move ask if an enemy rook can land on your back rank; if your king sits behind untouched f2-g2-h2, play h3/g3 or Kf1 first Example: 29.Rd4?? Rc1+ 30.Rd1 Rxd1 mate, 56%→2%
2. [endgame] When your rook can take an enemy rook with check, take it; fear of a "worse pawn race" never justifies declining a capture with check while material up Example: 28.Rd3+ instead of 28.Rxc7+ threw away 82%→54%
3. [endgame] Once an enemy rook reaches your second rank, answer with opposition on that rank (Rd2/Rd1) or Kh2 luft; do not drift sideways to guard a pawn Example: 30.Rb4?? "defending b2" allowed 30...Rc1 mate
4. [middlegame] Before every move, list enemy checks and captures, your loose pieces, and each bishop/rook/knight line hitting your destination square; if one wins material, choose otherwise Example: 13...Qc7?? Bxc7 (Bf4 guarded c7); 11.Bc4?? Qh4+ then Qxc4
5. [middlegame] Play each capture chain to the end, recounting attackers and defenders after every trade; a piece never defends the square it moves to Example: ...Qc3 "forked" rooks but bxc3 won the queen; 27...Rf5 was defended by the rook that moved
6. [middlegame] When a piece is attacked, consider pawn moves that counterattack or gain tempo, and test whether the opponent has a check that removes your retreat square Example: 25...Nd6? lost to 26.Qg5+ Kh7 27.Qxf6 then Qxd6; 25...c5 held
7. [middlegame] Before trading a minor piece, play out the recapture: if a pawn recapture opens a file or diagonal to your king or leaves pawns loose, keep the piece Example: 17...Nc4 18.Bxc4 dxc4 opened the e-file for Re4 and left c4 hanging, 51%→9%
8. [middlegame] Keep a piece on an advanced square only if a pawn can support it; retreat it before enemy pawns remove every escape square Example: Unsupported Ne4 had no retreat after e5/d6; Bd5 and Be4 were chased by pawns
9. [middlegame] Never push pawns in front of your castled king while an enemy bishop or queen eyes those squares; meet a wing storm with a central break Example: 17.g3 invited ...fxg3 and ...Bxh3; 17.dxc6 kept a winning game
10. [middlegame] When your rook can invade the enemy second rank hitting loose pawns, prefer that to any equal trade; with exposed kings, activity beats simplification Example: Instead of 17...Nc4, 17...Rf2 grabbing h2 and tying White down was winning
11. [endgame] With extra material and rooks on an open file, do not offer a trade to a defended enemy rook; insert a check first, then keep both rooks active Example: 34...Re2 allowed 35.Rxe2; 34...Re1+ drove the king back, 98% vs 91%
12. [endgame] With extra material, trade one-for-one but never your last extra piece; cut off his king, take the seventh rank, push a passer only with escort Example: Refusing 31.Rd5 dropped 94%→61%; 59...b5 pushed an unescorted pawn
13. [endgame] Keep extra pawns connected, stay inside the square of every passer, and before each move check enemy Kx-captures and stalemate tricks Example: 66...Qc3+? Kxc3 drew a won queen endgame; 29...dxc4 scattered a6/c4/c6
14. [opening] Delay the central break (d5/e5) until minors are developed and the king is castled; when two units can recapture, use the minor piece, not the queen Example: 8...d5 exd5 left a backward pawn; 10...Qxd5? 11.Qxd5 lost a tempo
15. [middlegame] When a bishop pins your knight against king or a loose rook, kick the pinner with a3/h3 or block with Bd2 instead of defending passively with the queen Example: 12.Qd2 let ...Nc5-e4 pile on; 12.a3 questioned Bb4 at once
