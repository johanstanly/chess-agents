# prompts — what the agents are told
The exact instructions sent to the AI agents, as plain text you can read and edit.

- `move_system.txt`, `move_turn.txt`: what Magnus and Hans are sent for every move (Magnus's also includes his notebook).
- `lessons_system.txt`, `lessons_turn.txt`: what Magnus is sent after a game he did not win, to write lessons.
- `merge_turn.txt`: what Magnus is sent when his notebook is full, to merge it down.
